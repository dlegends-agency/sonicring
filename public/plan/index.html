Billing questions

Short answers.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed monthly in US dollars. When you continue to checkout you're taken to a secure Stripe page to enter your email and card. Stripe processes the payment, so your card details never touch our servers.

Can I use a promo code?

Yes. If you have a promo code, enter it on the Stripe checkout page before you pay.

Which plan should I pick?

Most local contractors start with Growth. Not sure yet? Hear the AI receptionist answer a call for your business first, or start with Starter.

What if I need more than Pro?

Choose Enterprise and we'll put together a custom plan with AI tuned for your company, custom agents, members and storage.

Where can I read the full terms?

Billing, renewals and refunds are covered in our Terms & Conditions.
